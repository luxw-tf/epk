Bio

Sanakpaar is a duo out of Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh, built on the idea of crossing limits - "sanak" "paar". Sanakpaar isn't a translation, it's a philosophy born from the idea of pushing beyond the obsession. It represents the pursuit of growth beyond perceived limits - breaking creative boundaries, embracing chaos, and refusing to settle for convention.

Mady and YSH first met in school, reconnecting during the lockdown after independently beginning their rap journeys. Early collaborations revealed a rare chemistry, leading to the formation of Sanakpaar in 2025.

The duo crafts music that moves effortlessly between explosive club anthems, melodic introspection, and emotionally charged narratives. Rooted in Delhi NCR and shaped by both Indian musical heritage and global hip-hop influences, we're building more than a catalog - we're building a universe defined by chaos, ambition, and relentless evolution.

Sound & Influences

Chaos Crafted Into Music

Rooted in the Delhi NCR culture, the duo is redefining the boundaries of modern Indian rap through a fearless blend of trap, melody, and experimental production. Known for their versatility, Mady and YSH move effortlessly between high-energy bangers and emotionally driven records, creating a sound that is both cinematic and deeply personal.

Introduced to hip-hop through pioneers like Raftaar and Seedhe Maut, the duo later found their artistic direction in the sonic worlds of Travis Scott, Kanye West, and Drake - drawing from these influences while staying rooted in their own experiences, Sanakpaar is building a distinct identity-one driven by ambition, authenticity, and a vision to push Indian hip-hop beyond convention.

Lyrically, Mady and YSH explore ambition, identity, love, and the grind - writing from lived experience rather than borrowed narratives. The result is music that sounds cinematic but never loses its raw edge.

Live

Stage Presence

Sanakpaar's live performances are built on intensity, chemistry, and crowd connection. Blending commanding stage presence with high-energy delivery, the duo creates immersive sets that resonate long after the final track.

Within their first year, Sanakpaar has performed at 10+ live shows, collectively playing to audiences of 1,500+ people. They have also shared stages and opened for established names in the Indian hip-hop scene, including Arpit Bala, further strengthening their reputation as a compelling live act.

Whether delivering explosive rap records or melodic performances, Sanakpaar consistently brings authenticity, energy, and a performance-first mindset - earning enthusiastic responses from audiences at every show.
